Logo Design

Logo Design for InkMoon Games

I am a solo dev working on a videogame and created a Logo for myself. Here is a showcase of the Logo in its black and white, color, reverse and favicon versions.

The Logo Versions

1) Black and White

Single-color version for print, stamps and one-color uses.

2) Color Variation

The full-color logo used as the primary brand mark.

3) Reverse Version

Light logo designed to sit on dark backgrounds.

4) Favicon

Simplified mark used in the browser tab at small sizes.

Favicon Sizes

16 x 16

Browser tab icon. The smallest size, shown next to the page title.

32 x 32

Sharper tab icon on high-resolution screens, plus bookmarks and the taskbar.

48 x 48

Desktop shortcuts and site listings in search results.

180 x 180

Apple touch icon, used when the site is saved to an iPhone or iPad home screen.
