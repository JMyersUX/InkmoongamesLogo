Logo Design

Logo Design for InkMoon Games

I am a solo dev working on a videogame and created a Logo for myself. Here is a showcase of the Logo in its black and white, color, reverse and favicon versions.

Concept

The InkMoon Games logo shows a full moon with ink spilling across half of its surface and dripping off the edge. The moon represents imagination and atmosphere: a quiet, mysterious glow that draws players into new worlds. The ink represents creativity and craftsmanship, the hand-drawn, artistic process behind every game the studio makes.

The Logo Versions

1) Black and White

Single-color version for print, stamps and one-color uses.

2) Color Variation

The full-color logo used as the primary brand mark.

3) Reverse Version

Light logo designed to sit on dark backgrounds.

4) Favicon

Simplified mark used in the browser tab at small sizes.

Favicon Sizes

16 x 16

Browser tab icon. The smallest size, shown next to the page title.

32 x 32

Sharper tab icon on high-resolution screens, plus bookmarks and the taskbar.

48 x 48

Desktop shortcuts and site listings in search results.

180 x 180

Apple touch icon, used when the site is saved to an iPhone or iPad home screen.

Intended Use

These guidelines explain how to use the InkMoon Games logo so it stays clear and consistent everywhere it appears.

Sizes
  • Large (400px or wider): website headers, title screens, posters and banners, where the full logo with its text can be read.
  • Medium (120 to 400px): store pages, social media posts and menus. Keep the "InkMoon Games" text readable.
  • Small (under 120px): use the favicon instead of the full logo, since the text becomes too small to read.
  • Always scale the logo proportionally and leave empty space around it, at least the height of the "I" in "Ink".
Choosing a Version
  • Color: the main version. Use it on light backgrounds whenever full color is available.
  • Black and White: for print, stamps, documents and any place limited to one color.
  • Reverse: for dark backgrounds, photos and the dark mode of a page. Pick it whenever the dark logo would blend in.
  • Favicon: for browser tabs, app icons and any space too small for the full logo.
File Formats
  • Use a vector format first (SVG): it stays sharp at every size, from a tab icon to a billboard, and has a small file size.
  • Use PNG only where SVG is not supported.
  • Avoid JPG for the logo. It has no transparent background and adds blur around the edges.
Don'ts
  • Don't change the colors of the logo.
  • Don't add effects such as shadows, glows, outlines or gradients.
  • Don't overlap the logo with text or other graphics.
  • Don't stretch, squash, rotate or crop the logo.
  • Don't place a version on a background that makes it hard to see, such as the color logo on a dark photo.
